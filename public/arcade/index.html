⚡ How it works

Your score is your distance plus 10 for every coin (⚡ boost doubles it for a few seconds). You start slow, but the tunnel keeps getting faster. Ride up the walls and all the way round: the camera turns with you. Press S to teleport straight across the tunnel, handy when the gap is on the other side! 🧲 pulls coins to you · 🛡️ saves you from one hit · ⚡ smashes through everything. The 3 worlds only change the look: everyone’s on one leaderboard.
