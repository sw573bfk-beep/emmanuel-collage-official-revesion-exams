EC Revisions

Privacy and safety

What EC Revisions saves, who can see it, and how to delete it. In plain English.

EC Revisions was made by a student (Beau). It is not an official Emmanuel College website and isn’t run by the school.

What is saved

  • Your name, the one you sign up with. Please use your real first and last name so friends and Beau know who you are. No email address or phone number is needed.
  • Your password, scrambled (hashed) so nobody, not even Beau, can read it.
  • Your progress: practice scores, booklet marks, checklists, flashcards and exam results, so they follow you to any device.
  • When you study: how long you have Maths or Science open and active, and whether you use a phone, tablet or computer. This is how the leaderboard counts minutes.
  • Messages: chats with friends, messages to Beau and feedback.

A login cookie keeps you signed in. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Who can see what

WhoWhat they can see
YouEverything in your own account.
Other studentsYour name and points on the leaderboard, your name in friend search and games, and messages you send them in a chat.
Beau (the site’s admin)Names, progress, study time, and every message: chats between friends and messages to Beau. This is to keep everyone safe.

Chat safety

  • A chat only starts when both students agree to be friends and Beau approves it.
  • Beau can read every chat, and can end any chat at any time.
  • Chat messages between friends are deleted automatically after 21 days.
  • Rude words and personal details (phone numbers, addresses, social media) are blocked automatically.
  • Tap Report on any message that worries you, or Block someone. Tell a teacher or a parent if something online upsets you.
  • Beau can turn on study mode, which turns chat off so you can focus.

Deleting your account

Go to the home page, open Account settings and tap Delete account. Your account, progress, study time and messages are deleted for good. If you can’t log in, ask Beau to delete it for you.
