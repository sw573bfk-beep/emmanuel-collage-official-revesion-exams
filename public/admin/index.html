What each admin level can do

🛡️ Basic admin can only restrict: read reported messages, 1-on-1 chats and what the filter blocked; delete messages; say Not now to chats or end them; pause chatting; put people into study mode (or everyone); read feedback and mark it done.

⭐ Top tier admin can do all of that, plus approve chats, let people chat again, take people out of study mode and restore deleted messages.

Neither can see your messages, delete accounts, reset passwords, unlock the booklet, make codes, send pop-ups, or see Security, usage or everyone’s stats. Only you can remove an admin level.

Every sign-in to your account, and every wrong password typed for it. A device only gets 5 wrong tries an hour on your account. If you see wrong tries you don’t recognise, someone is guessing: use a long password only you know.

Your Cloudflare plan:

Daily limits reset at 10:00 am Queensland time (midnight UTC). If a daily limit runs out on the Free plan, the site stops working for everyone until then. Limits are from Cloudflare’s pricing pages (check dash.cloudflare.com if they change).
